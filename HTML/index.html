About Director

S. S. Rajamouli is one of India’s most celebrated film directors, known for his grand vision and innovative storytelling. Starting his career in Telugu cinema, he quickly rose to fame with unique and powerful films. His movies often combine history, fantasy, and emotion in a way that captivates audiences. Magadheera showcased his flair for epic narratives and strong characters. With Baahubali, he set global benchmarks, making Indian cinema known worldwide. His recent masterpiece RRR won international awards and further strengthened his reputation. Rajamouli is admired for his use of technology, large-scale production, and breathtaking visuals. He consistently raises the standards of filmmaking in India. His stories highlight themes of courage, sacrifice, and cultural richness. Today, Rajamouli stands as a symbol of Indian cinema’s creativity and global success.

About Music Director

M. M. Keeravani is a highly respected Indian music composer and playback singer, best known for his work in Telugu cinema. He has created memorable melodies that combine classical depth with modern style. His music career spans several decades, with hit songs in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, and other languages. Keeravani has worked on many iconic films, giving soulful tunes that stay in people’s hearts. His collaboration with S. S. Rajamouli has produced unforgettable albums, including the music for Baahubali and RRR. The background score of these films added immense power and emotion to the storytelling. He has received numerous awards, including national and international recognition, for his contribution to music. Keeravani’s style blends Indian traditions with global sounds, making his music unique. He continues to inspire young musicians and remains a pillar of the Indian film music industry. His legacy is defined by timeless songs and powerful background scores that elevate cinema.
